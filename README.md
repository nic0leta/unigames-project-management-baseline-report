# UniGames – University Sports Tournament Project Management

## Overview

This repository contains an academic project management baseline report for **UniGames**, a university sports tournament planned to take place in Thessaloniki.

The project focuses on the organization of a sports event between universities, with the goal of promoting teamwork, sportsmanship, student participation, and social contribution.

All revenue from the tournament is planned to support the association **“Achtida”**, which helps families with children on the autism spectrum.

## Project Purpose

The purpose of UniGames is to organize a university sports tournament that strengthens the student community and encourages collaboration between students from different universities.

The tournament includes popular sports such as:

* Football
* Basketball
* Volleyball
* Tennis
* Track and field

The project aims to combine sports, entertainment, teamwork, and charity.

## Main Objectives

The main objectives of the project are:

* Organize a university sports tournament
* Promote sportsmanship and cooperation between universities
* Encourage student participation
* Raise money for the association “Achtida”
* Create a well-structured and realistic event management plan
* Define project roles, responsibilities, risks, costs, and deadlines

## Revenue Goal

The project estimates revenue from two main sources:

### Tickets and Participation Fees

* Entrance fee: 3€ per spectator
* Participation fee: 2€ per player
* Maximum number of players: 200
* Maximum number of spectators: 500

Estimated revenue:

```text
200 x 2€ + 500 x 3€ = 1,900€
```

### Sponsorships

* Number of sponsors: 10
* Small businesses: 300€
* Large businesses: 500€

Estimated sponsorship revenue:

```text
6 x 300€ + 4 x 500€ = 3,800€
```

### Total Target Revenue

```text
1,900€ + 3,800€ = 5,700€
```

The team planned regular weekly meetings to monitor progress and assign upcoming tasks.

Communication and coordination tools included:

* Zoom Meetings
* Google Calendar
* Google Drive
* Microsoft Word
* Microsoft PowerPoint

The team also followed a decision-making process based on discussion, proposal of solutions, internal voting, and additional argumentation in case of a tie.

## Project Scope

### In Scope

The project includes:

* Organization of a university sports tournament
* Fundraising for “Achtida”
* Promotion of sportsmanship and cooperation between universities
* Sponsorship management
* Medical support during the games
* Reservation of sports facilities
* Provision of sports equipment
* Cooperation with universities and student services
* Creation of rules and competition documents
* Creation of the tournament schedule
* Formation of an administrative event team

### Out of Scope

The project does not include:

* Construction of new sports facilities
* Long-term management of partnerships
* Sports lessons or educational training programs
* Transportation of athletes
* Transportation of equipment
* Responsibility for supplier-related problems

## Project Management Components

The report includes several core project management elements:

### Work Breakdown Structure

A WBS was created to break the project into smaller work packages, such as:

* Project planning
* Permissions and approvals
* Venue and equipment organization
* Sponsorships
* Promotion and registrations
* Tournament schedule organization
* Project closure and evaluation

### Gantt Chart

A Gantt chart was used to plan and visualize the project timeline, task durations, dependencies, and deadlines.

### Milestones and Deliverables

The project defines key milestones and deliverables, including:

* Team formation
* Project manager assignment
* Kick-off meeting
* Scope definition
* Budget planning
* Risk estimation
* Venue reservations
* Sponsorship agreements
* Promotion activities
* Registrations
* Tournament schedule
* Final event evaluation

### RAM / RACI Matrix

A responsibility assignment matrix was used to define the involvement of each team member in the project tasks.

### Resource Usage Chart

A resource usage chart was included to monitor how the team members and project resources are allocated across the project timeline.

### Risk Management

The report includes risk identification and risk management planning.

Examples of possible risks include:

* Reduced athlete participation
* Reduced volunteer participation
* Problems in the tournament schedule
* Resource shortages
* Delays in sponsorships or permissions

### Cost Analysis

The project includes an analysis of variable and fixed costs, as well as the total project cost.

The total variable cost is estimated at:

```text
2,366.50€
```

The cost planning section supports the financial feasibility of the event and helps ensure better use of available resources.
